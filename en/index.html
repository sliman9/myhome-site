Design your home before you get the keys

Draw your floor plan, furnish every room and walk through it in 3D with real sunlight. MyHome works out the areas, materials and finishing costs for you.

Floor plans to scaleWalls, windows, doors, stairs, roofs and garden, across several floors.
FurnishingNearly 200 pieces of furniture in seven styles, with your own sizes, colours and materials.
3D and photo modeOrbit around your home or walk through it, with sunlight for every hour of the year.
Finishing and handoverQuantities, costs, PDFs to scale and a snag list with photos.

Everything stays on your Mac

No account, no ads, no tracking and no internet needed. Your designs, photos and notes stay on your own Mac. Read more in the privacy policy.
