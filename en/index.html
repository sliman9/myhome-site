MyHome

Design your home on your Mac

Draw your floor plan, furnish every room and see it all in 3D or as a photorealistic image. MyHome works out areas, walls, floors and finishing costs, makes PDFs to scale and keeps a snag list.

Your designs stay on your own Mac. If you want help from Claude (AI), you connect your own Anthropic API key.
