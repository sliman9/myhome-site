Help and contact

A question, problem or idea? Email sulaimanh1996@icloud.com. Please mention your macOS version and the MyHome version (see the question mark at the top right of the app).

Frequently asked questions

Where are my projects?
Only on your Mac, inside the app. Make a backup now and then via Projects → Back up this project. You restore it with Import backup, also on another Mac.

How do I connect Claude?
Create an API key at console.anthropic.com and add some credit there. In MyHome open “Ask Claude”, paste the key and give your consent. You pay Anthropic per use; MyHome charges nothing extra.

Claude says my key doesn't work.
Check that the key still exists and that there is credit in the Anthropic Console. Remove the key in MyHome and enter it again.

A web shop link doesn't work.
Some shops block automatic fetching. Take a screenshot of the product page (photo and dimensions) and paste it into MyHome.

The 3D view or photo mode is slow.
Close other heavy apps, and use the normal 3D view while editing; photo mode is for the best-looking image.

Can I switch the language?
MyHome follows the language of your Mac: Dutch when Dutch is your first language in System Settings → General → Language & Region, otherwise English. You can also choose a language for MyHome alone under Applications in that same settings screen.
