Privacy policy

MyHome for Mac · last updated: 6 November 2026

In short: MyHome collects no data about you. Your designs stay on your own Mac. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your Mac

Your projects, floor plans, furniture, photos, notes and settings are only stored locally, in the app's protected storage on your Mac. We (the developer) cannot access them. You make a backup yourself via Projects; you keep that file wherever you like.

Claude (optional)

MyHome can use Claude, the AI model from Anthropic. This only works if you enter your own Anthropic API key yourself and explicitly give your consent. When you ask Claude something, the following goes directly from your Mac to Anthropic:

  • your question and the conversation so far;
  • a summary of the open project (rooms, dimensions, furniture);
  • an image, if you add one yourself.

This goes through your own Anthropic account, under Anthropic's privacy policy and terms. We receive or keep none of it. Your API key is stored encrypted in your Mac's keychain and is only sent to Anthropic. “Remove key” deletes it.

Products from web shops (optional)

If you paste a link to a product, MyHome fetches that page and the product photos directly from that web shop, to take over the name, dimensions and photos. The web shop sees an ordinary visit. Nothing is sent to us.

Links

Links to web shops or other sites open in your own browser. What happens there falls under that site's policy.

Children

MyHome is not aimed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If anything changes in how MyHome handles data, we update this policy and you will see the new date at the top.
