Privacy policy

MyHome for Mac · last updated: 7 November 2026

In short: MyHome collects no data about you and does not connect to the internet. Your designs stay on your own Mac. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your Mac

Your projects, floor plans, furniture, photos, notes and settings are only stored locally, in the app's protected storage on your Mac. We (the developer) cannot access them. You make a backup yourself via Projects; you keep that file wherever you like.

Links

If you add a product link to a piece of furniture yourself, it opens in your own browser. What happens there falls under that site's policy. MyHome itself fetches nothing from the internet.

Children

MyHome is not aimed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If anything changes in how MyHome handles data, we update this policy and you will see the new date at the top.
